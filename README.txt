SONIA AI RECEPTIONIST - WINDOWS 11
Deepika Computer & Printer Center (DCPC-0001)

INSTALLATION
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click Install_Sonia_AI.bat
4. A desktop shortcut named "Sonia AI Receptionist" will be created.
5. Open the shortcut.

MICROPHONE
- Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
- Click the red microphone button.
- Allow microphone permission when asked.
- Internet may be required by Chrome/Edge for voice recognition.
- Typed questions and offline replies work without internet.

UNINSTALL
Delete these two items:
1. Desktop shortcut: Sonia AI Receptionist
2. Folder: C:\Users\YOUR-NAME\Sonia AI Receptionist
